## How this documentation stays current

Two gates, because staleness has two shapes.

**Change-based** — `tools/check-consistency.py` re-runs every generator in `--check` mode and
fails when a derived artifact disagrees with its source: `docs/api/`, `docs/portal/portal-data.js`
and `web/pack-index.js`. `docs-graph.py validate` fails on invalid frontmatter, dangling typed
links, entries whose files are gone, and index-versus-frontmatter disagreement. Both run in
`tools/verify-bundle.ps1` and in CI on every push.

**Time-based** — `.github/workflows/docs.yml` runs weekly, because an artifact's `review-by`
lapses on a *date*, not on a commit: on a quiet week nothing pushes and nothing notices. It fails
on a push or PR and warns on the schedule, so a review-by that lapsed overnight raises a signal
for that artifact's owner without reddening `main` for everyone. `docs-graph.py snapshot` records
the graph-health trend that makes governance drift visible as a series.

Coverage, confidence labels, and the known gaps are recorded in
[`_meta.json`](_meta.json) — including the largest open debt, that 60% of the deployed bundle's
public functions still carry no docstring.
